The founder's note

Why Subeazy exists

Premium tools like LinkedIn Premium, Canva Pro, and today's best AI subscriptions are priced for a different market than the one most of us shop in. Subeazy started as a way to close that gap honestly — sourcing genuine plans through eligible offers and passing the savings straight to you.

No shared logins, no black-box "cracked" accounts, no vanishing after payment. Every order goes through a real conversation on WhatsApp with a real person on the other end — because trust is the whole business model here, not an afterthought.

We'd rather stay small and transparent about exactly what you're buying than grow fast on promises we can't keep.

What "genuine" means here

  • ✓ Real, activatable subscriptions — not shared credentials
  • ✓ Clear pricing, no hidden renewal traps
  • ✓ A person you can actually message if something's off
  • ✓ Honest limits — we'll tell you if a plan isn't a fit
